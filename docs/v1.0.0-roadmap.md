## 2. Homepage redesign: AI literacy and advocacy

**Status**: `done` (landed in commit `c004d3e`)

`src/pages/index.astro` rebuilt around the site's new focus. See [`architecture/site.md`](architecture/site.md#homepage) for how it is put together.

- **Two-dangers layout.** The thesis is quoted, not reprinted: a hero line ("The tools arrived before the lessons."), then two columns, "The familiar kind" and "The second kind", each with a quote and a short line from the thesis, then a closing "trust" section that links the full statement at `/writing/why-dangerous-robot-exists`.
- **Visible menu.** A "Where to start" list with seven deep links (Writing, Turn off AI, Should I use AI?, AI Safety Index, Responsible AI, Research, Values), each with a label and a one-line note. The hamburger stays and shows the same seven links from the same array.
- **Two placed claim cards replace the random scatter.** One published claim sits under each danger column, chosen by preferred claim id with a fallback to the `highlight`-tagged pool. The build-time randomized card scatter is gone.
- **Stale copy removed.** Page title "AI Transparency Research", the "a transparency index" hero meta line, and the "research hub for journalists, researchers, and policy teams" JSON-LD description are replaced.
- **Site-wide nav unchanged by decision.** `src/layouts/Base.astro` navigation was intentionally left as it was; only the homepage changed.

Since then (beta.4, 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-06): a new tagline and hero line, a spotlight band for one featured entry, the danger columns headed "Two kinds of AI danger", and a four-entry "Where to start" menu (Research, Turn off AI and Should I use AI left it). See §9 and [`decisions.md`](decisions.md).
