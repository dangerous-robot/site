## RE-AD detail: Evaluator grades the claim

- `_graded_statement(template, entity_name, claim_text, analyst_title)` returns the analyst's title when it passes `validate_analyst_title`, `None` when it fails, and the operator's `claim_text` when there is no template (`pipeline/orchestrator/pipeline.py:1513-1533`).
- `verify_claim` calls it after the analyst (`:522-524`); `None` logs "Auditor skipped" and sets `comparison = None` (`:525-531`); otherwise the cited filter runs and `_audit_claim` gets `statement` (`:532-538`).
- `research_claim` (`dr claim-draft`) grades `analyst_out.verdict.title`, the title it writes (`:1836-1842`).
- `dr step-audit` builds the bundle with `claim_text=fm["title"]` (`pipeline/orchestrator/cli.py:1618-1627`) and skips `status: blocked` (`:1581-1585`).
- `ClaimBundle.claim_text` is documented as the thing graded (`pipeline/auditor/models.py:33-36`); `build_auditor_prompt` emits `## Claim` before `## Analyst narrative (context; not the thing you grade)` (`pipeline/auditor/agent.py:40-51`); instructions rule at `pipeline/auditor/instructions.md:16-22`.
- `compare.py` unchanged by the fix (roadmap `docs/v1.0.0-roadmap.md:239`).
- Known gap recorded in the backlog: `dr claim-draft` no longer compares the analyst's title with the operator's text (`docs/UNSCHEDULED.md:416`); `dr claim-refresh` without templates grades the old title (`:415`).

## RE-AE detail: Source pool independence

## RE-AI detail: archive lookup order

1. TimeGate (`check_archive_org_timegate`, `wayback.py:128-174`).
2. `check_capture` HEADs the `id_` replay, follows at most two captured redirects that stay on the page (`:258-321`).
3. On rejection, `latest_ok_capture` queries CDX with `filter=statuscode:200` (`:324-373`); backlog notes CDX is slow (`docs/UNSCHEDULED.md:426`).
4. No candidate and saves allowed and no 429: `_save_and_check` (`:376-390`).
5. Candidate stale (`_is_stale`, `:393-405`) and saves allowed: refresh, keep old on failure (`:466-478`).
6. `archive_after_ingest` passes freshness only after a live fetch; a model-returned link is reused when the text came from an archive copy or a prefetched body (`ingestor/agent.py:280-290`).
