**Evidence Summary:**

- The Future of Life Institute's latest AI Safety Index, the Summer 2026 edition, graded nine AI labs. Existential Safety was the weakest domain, "with no lab scoring above D+" (*AI Safety Index Summer 2026: FLI Grades Nine Labs, Tops Out at C+*; *AI Safety Index — Summer 2026*).
- The two 2025 editions gave lower grades in the same domain: in Summer 2025, "No company achieved a score above a D in this domain for the second consecutive edition" (*AI Safety Report: Top 3 Companies Outpace Rivals In Risk*; *2025 AI Safety Index*).
- The labs graded include Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind and Meta, the major AI model producers the claim refers to.

**Conclusion:** No major AI model producer scores above D+ on existential safety in the latest FLI AI Safety Index, and none scored above D in the 2025 editions before it.

No contradictory evidence was found.
