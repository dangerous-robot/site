OpenAI’s own documentation states that it **does not train its models on user data by default** and gives users control over data usage, contradicting the claim that OpenAI trains chats by default. Anthropic’s privacy analysis and its updated consumer terms both indicate that Anthropic uses an **opt‑in** model for data training, so its chats are **not used by default**, again contradicting the claim. An independent report on Google’s Gemini assistant explains that the service **automatically learns from user chats unless the user disables the setting**, and it notes that the feature is unavailable only in the EU, UK, and Switzerland, implying that U.S. users are included; this supports the claim’s statement about Google training by default, although no source explicitly tags the United States. Meta’s opt‑out guide and a CNBC article explain that **U.S. users cannot effectively opt out of Meta’s AI training**, confirming the claim that Meta offers no opt‑out in the United States. No source directly addresses whether Google’s default training policy applies specifically to U.S. users, leaving a coverage gap for that sub‑question.
