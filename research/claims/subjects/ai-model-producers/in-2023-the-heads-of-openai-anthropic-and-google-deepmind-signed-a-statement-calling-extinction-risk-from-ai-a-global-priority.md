Multiple independent sources confirm that in May 2023 the CEOs of OpenAI (Sam Altman), Anthropic, and Google DeepMind (Demis Hassabis) co‑signed the *Statement on AI Extinction Risk* released by the Center for AI Safety. The statement’s core language reads, “Mitigating the risk of extinction from AI should be a global priority alongside other societal‑scale risks such as pandemics and nuclear war.” The joint signing is reported in a Wikipedia article on the statement, a Business Insider piece describing the CEOs’ joint declaration, and a Center for AI Safety press release that lists the three leaders among the signatories. No sources report any disputes over the authenticity or signatory list, indicating consensus on the event’s occurrence.
