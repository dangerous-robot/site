Independent reporting estimates that U.S. data centers consumed about 192 TWh of grid electricity in 2024 (*Electric Choice*). A Pew Research Center analysis finds natural gas supplied over 40 % of that electricity while renewables accounted for roughly 24 % (*Pew Research Center*). These shares imply that gas‑derived electricity (about 77 TWh) exceeded renewable‑derived electricity (about 46 TWh) in 2024, consistent with the claim. No source in the pool provides a direct measurement or model that quantifies the exact gas‑vs‑renewables gap for the sector in 2024, so that specific comparison is not directly documented. Major providers such as Equinix publicly report high renewable energy coverage—96 % renewable in 2024 (*Equinix Sustainability Data Summary*)—but this provider‑level disclosure does not counter the aggregate finding for U.S. data centers as a whole. No third‑party audit was found that disputes the gas‑dominance estimate, leaving that aspect unaddressed.
