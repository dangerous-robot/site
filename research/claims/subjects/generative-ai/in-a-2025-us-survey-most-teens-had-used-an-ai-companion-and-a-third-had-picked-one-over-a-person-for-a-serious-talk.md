Two independent surveys of U.S. teens in 2025 report that a clear majority had used AI companions and about one‑third chose an AI companion over a human for serious conversations. The Common Sense Media report notes that "a third of teens have chosen AI companions over humans for serious conversations" and that roughly three‑quarters of teens engage with such tools. A Fast Company‑sourced article quoting the same research finds that 72 % of teens have used an AI companion at least once and that 31 % say their AI conversations are as satisfying—or more satisfying—than those with people. No source in the pool raises methodological criticisms or disputes about these surveys, leaving that aspect unaddressed.
