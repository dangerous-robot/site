Google’s own measurements report that a median Gemini text prompt consumes 0.24 watt‑hours (Wh) of electricity, and an independent MIT Technology Review article confirms this figure (*Google Reveals Energy Use of Gemini AI Prompts*). A Tech Insider analysis notes that OpenAI’s ChatGPT uses roughly 42 % more energy per query than Gemini, putting its per‑prompt consumption at about 0.34 Wh – close to a third of a watt‑hour (*ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Claude: 42% Energy Gap*). These numbers support the claim’s estimate of energy use for a typical prompt. However, none of the sources directly compare the prompt’s energy to the operation of an LED bulb; the available analogies reference short TV viewing periods rather than LED minutes. Consequently, while the energy‑use figure is well‑substantiated, the LED‑bulb analogy lacks evidential backing, leading to a mixed assessment.
