Google’s own measurements and independent reporting show that a median Gemini text prompt consumes **0.24 watt‑hours (Wh)** of electricity (*Google Reveals Energy Use of Gemini AI Prompts*), while several independent analyses of OpenAI’s service place a typical ChatGPT query at **≈0.34 Wh** (*The True Price of Every ChatGPT Prompt*; *ChatGPT Energy Usage Is 0.34 Wh Per Query*). Both figures are close to one‑third of a watt‑hour, matching the claim’s energy estimate. A 5‑10 W LED bulb draws about 0.083‑0.167 Wh per minute, so 0.24‑0.34 Wh corresponds to roughly 2‑4 minutes of LED illumination, supporting the “couple of minutes” analogy. Independent benchmarks comparing the two models (e.g., the 42 % higher energy use of ChatGPT versus Gemini) corroborate these per‑prompt numbers (*ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Claude: 42% Energy Gap*). No source found evidence that a third of a watt‑hour is inconsistent with the infrastructure or scaling reports for either model, leaving that sub‑question unaddressed.
