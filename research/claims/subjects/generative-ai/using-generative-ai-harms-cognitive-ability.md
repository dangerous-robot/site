Multiple independent studies report that generative AI use reduces cognitive engagement, lowers mental effort, and can lead to poorer memory and critical‑thinking performance. The article *Generative AI: the risk of cognitive atrophy* summarizes research showing decreased cognitive load when participants write with ChatGPT. A Stanford report (*ChatGPT Produces More “Lazy” Thinkers*) found significantly lower cognitive engagement scores in AI‑assisted writers. MIT Media Lab’s study (*Your Brain on ChatGPT*) documented reduced brain connectivity and memory recall among LLM users over several months. An Elsevier review (*AI‑overdependence and human cognitive decline*) highlights growing concerns about AI‑driven deskilling, and a BBC Future piece (*AI Chatbots Could Be Making You Stupider*) warns of reduced activation in brain areas tied to creativity and processing. Together, these sources demonstrate that generative AI can indeed harm cognitive ability.
