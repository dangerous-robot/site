Multiple independent reports document that by mid‑2026 more than a thousand court proceedings worldwide involved AI‑generated or "invented" citations. One article cites 1,598 such cases, another dataset lists 2,217, and a third notes over 1,400 instances. Courts have also imposed sanctions: a California attorney was fined $10,000, a Mississippi federal judge suspended two attorneys for two years, and other federal and state courts have issued monetary penalties for AI‑produced fake citations. No third‑party database or monitoring service that systematically tracks these incidents was identified, leaving a gap in comprehensive reporting. Likewise, sources do not mention any retractions or disputed cases where AI‑generated citations were later corrected, indicating that the reported filings remain largely undisputed.
