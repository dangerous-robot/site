Google’s own documentation – the blog article *How much energy does Google’s AI use? We did the math* and the report *Measuring the Environmental Impact of Delivering AI at Google Scale* – state that the median Gemini text prompt consumes roughly 0.24 watt‑hours of electricity. Several independent news pieces (*Carbon Footprint of AI*, *How Much Energy Do Your AI Prompts Consume? Google Shares Its Gemini Findings*, *Your AI prompts come with an energy price tag—Google just revealed how high*, and *Quantifying Energy Consumption in AI Systems: A Case Study of Google Gemini*) repeat this figure but do not present original measurements, merely restating Google’s disclosed number. Because the independent sources provide no original analysis, the verification rests on Google’s first‑party materials, leading to a self‑reported verification level and low confidence, though the claim is directly supported by the available evidence.
