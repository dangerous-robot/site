Independent analyses estimate that a single generative AI prompt consumes between 0.24 and 0.34 watt‑hours of electricity (*The Hidden Behemoth Behind Every AI Answer*, *Energy Use of AI Inference: Efficiency Pathways and Test‑Time Compute*, *Google Reveals Energy Use of Gemini AI Prompts*). However, none of the provided sources quantify the electricity used by a typical web search, leaving the central comparison in the claim unsupported. Because the evidence does not address the web‑search baseline, we cannot verify the claim that AI prompts use roughly ten times more electricity than a web search.
