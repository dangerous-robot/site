Evidence on the electricity required for a generative‑AI prompt varies widely. A Kanoppi article reports that a ChatGPT‑style query consumes about 0.0029 kWh versus 0.0003 kWh for a traditional Google search, a roughly ten‑fold increase *Search Engines vs AI: Energy Consumption Compared*. By contrast, the AIMultiple report notes a 4–5 × multiplier, while Scientific American and an arXiv study cite 30 × and 60–70 × higher consumption respectively *AI Energy Consumption Statistics*, *What Do Google’s AI Answers Cost the Environment?*, *Estimating the Increase in Emissions caused by AI-augmented Search*. The divergent estimates mean the claim that answering a generative‑AI prompt uses “roughly ten times” the electricity of a web search is only partially supported.
