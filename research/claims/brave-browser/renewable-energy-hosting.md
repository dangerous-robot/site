Sources reveal that Brave Browser currently runs on AWS servers (Source 2, 4), which does not guarantee renewable energy because AWS’s cloud mix includes non-renewable sources. Third-party audits or green certifications focusing on Brave’s infrastructure were not found. Brave's FAQ and website (Sources 4, 8) emphasize privacy and ethical practices but do not publicly commit to renewable energy for hosting. External evaluations or independent reviews narrowly addressing its hosting sustainability were not located. While Brave's ecosystem promotes ethical and privacy-respecting approaches, direct evidence that its web hosting runs on 100% renewable energy remains unavailable. Sources 6 and 7 mention technological integration, crowdfunding endeavors, and decentralized web architecture, but these do not address the claim about hosting energy sources. Given the overwhelming use of AWS (Source 2), it is plausible that non-renewable energy is among the sources used.
