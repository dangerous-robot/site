Meta’s updated privacy policy and multiple independent reports state that the company harvests public and private user‑generated content from Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and other services to train its AI models, with no universal opt‑out for U.S. users and only limited opt‑out mechanisms for EU/UK users *Meta Is Using Your Photos to Train Its AI*; *Meta Faces Privacy Concerns Over Plans to Scrape Facebook Data for AI Training*. Guidance on opting out confirms that U.S. users cannot effectively prevent their data from being used, while European users must submit a form to object *How to Opt Out of Meta’s AI Training*. Class‑action lawsuits in the United States allege that Meta used personal photos and messages for AI training without consent, reinforcing that user data is indeed employed *Meta Faces Class Action Lawsuit Over Use of Personal Data to Train AI Systems*; *Meta Sued Over Illegal Use of Biometric Data for AI and Face‑Recognition Systems*.
