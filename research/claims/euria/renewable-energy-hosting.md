Infomaniak’s own communications state that Euria runs in its Swiss data centres powered by renewable energy, and they highlight that the AI’s energy is recycled to heat homes (*Euria: the Free, Sovereign AI Assistant to Challenge American Dominance*; *Infomaniak Launches Euria: Free Sovereign AI Respecting Privacy and Powered by Renewables*). Independent coverage also notes the renewable‑energy claim, with PrivacyTools.io describing Euria as using renewable energy for its operations (*Euria: A Private AI Assistant Hosted in Switzerland*). The hosting provider is Infomaniak itself, which operates locally in Switzerland, and no third‑party ESG or sustainability ratings were found to assess its renewable‑energy practices, leaving a coverage gap for sub‑question sq2.
