This blog post details Infomaniak's actions to reduce its environmental impact, such as extending server lifespans, investing in renewable energy, and offsetting CO2 emissions at 200%. Despite these efforts, the company acknowledges ongoing challenges and advocates for stricter legal frameworks to improve sustainability in data centers. The post emphasizes transparency and continuous improvement.
