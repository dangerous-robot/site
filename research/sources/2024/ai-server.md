OVHcloud's page outlines its AI server hosting solutions, featuring bare-metal servers equipped with NVIDIA GPUs, AMD EPYC processors, and high-bandwidth networking. The documentation highlights use cases like deep learning, NLP, and LLMs, emphasizing performance, scalability, sustainability, and full root-access flexibility. It also provides comparisons of server models (Scale-GPU-1, Scale-GPU-2, Scale-GPU-3, and HGR-AI-2) with specifications, pricing ($1,145–$4,355/month), and deployment options.
