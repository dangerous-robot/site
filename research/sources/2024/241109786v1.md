This report presents a comprehensive analysis of the environmental impact of 2,132 U.S. data centers operating between September 2023 and August 2024. The study highlights their significant electricity consumption (4.59% of U.S. total) and carbon dioxide equivalent (CO2e) emissions, primarily driven by fossil fuels. The report also underscores the challenges in regulating these emissions due to discrepancies between energy consumption locations and power generation hubs. Additionally, it discusses the role of AI-driven demand in exacerbating energy use and emissions.
