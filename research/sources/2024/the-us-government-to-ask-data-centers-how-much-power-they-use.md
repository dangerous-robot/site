The article explains that the US Energy Information Administration (EIA) will launch the first mandatory nationwide survey of data centers to gather energy consumption data, following requests from Senators Elizabeth Warren and Josh Hawley. The pilot surveys in Texas, Washington, and Virginia/DC aim to establish a framework for broader data collection, addressing concerns about rising energy costs and environmental impacts tied to data center operations.
