The article examines how AI's rapid expansion is contributing to environmental challenges, such as the energy demands of data centers and their reliance on non-renewable resources. While AI has potential applications like optimizing renewable energy and reducing waste, the lack of transparency from major companies and the risk of increased energy demand pose significant concerns. Viro AI is presented as an alternative that funds clean energy projects to mitigate these impacts.
