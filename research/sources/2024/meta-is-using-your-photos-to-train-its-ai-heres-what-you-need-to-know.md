Meta updated its privacy policy in June 2024, permitting the use of user-generated data (such as posts, photos, and videos) for AI training. While EU and UK users have limited opt-out options under GDPR regulations, US users lack any clear method to prevent their data from being used. The article recommends limiting public exposure, deleting sensitive content, and using privacy-focused tools.
