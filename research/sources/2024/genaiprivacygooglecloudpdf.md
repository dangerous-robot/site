Google Cloud’s 2024 whitepaper details the company’s privacy-by-design approach to generative AI, including assurances that customer data will not be used for model training without explicit permission. It also outlines commitments to data control, transparency, and adherence to regulations like GDPR. Additional commitments extend to Workspace and education users, ensuring strict privacy protections.
