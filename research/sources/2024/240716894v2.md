This report compares energy consumption and carbon emissions between traditional search queries and AI-augmented search, using updated data for conventional search efficiency and estimates for AI models like BLOOM and ChatGPT. It concludes that AI-augmented search queries consume significantly more energy and contribute disproportionately higher emissions.
