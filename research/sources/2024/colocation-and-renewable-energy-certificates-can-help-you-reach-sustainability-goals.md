This article discusses how colocation and renewable energy certificates (RECs) contribute to sustainability in technology-driven enterprises. It explains how colocation consolidates energy-efficient infrastructure, enabling smaller operations to reduce their carbon footprint. RECs allow businesses to offset emissions by claiming credits for renewable energy sources like wind, solar, and hydroelectric power, even without direct access to these resources.
