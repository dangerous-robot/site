Google's AI Overviews, powered by the Gemini large language model, delivers instant answers to search queries. However, this convenience comes with a steep environmental and financial toll. Studies estimate AI-driven searches generate **19 kilograms of CO2 daily**, equivalent to driving 49 miles in an average car, and consume energy at a rate **30 times higher** than traditional searches. While Google claims costs have decreased due to efficiency improvements, the tech giant continues to expand its data centers, relying on increasingly strained fossil fuel resources. Analysts warn of long-term sustainability challenges, including misaligned renewable energy production and stagnant carbon-free energy adoption rates.
