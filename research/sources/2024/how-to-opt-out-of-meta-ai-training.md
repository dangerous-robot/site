This article explains how users can opt out of Meta's AI training systems, focusing on differences between US users (no effective opt-out) and users in the EU/UK (form-based objection process). It outlines step-by-step instructions for Facebook and Instagram users in the EU/UK to submit objections via Meta’s privacy settings.
