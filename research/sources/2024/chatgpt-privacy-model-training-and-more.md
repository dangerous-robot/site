This BYU article explains how users can disable ChatGPT's use of their conversations for model training, both for logged-in and logged-out users. It highlights step-by-step instructions for navigating settings and provides guidance on using temporary chats, which prevent data usage for training purposes while still allowing chat functionality. The article retains screenshots to assist users in following the process.
