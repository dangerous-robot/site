The advocacy group NOYB filed complaints in 11 European countries against Meta's planned policy change, which would allow the platform to scrape user data—including private posts, images, and tracking data—without explicit opt-in consent. This move is seen as violating EU data privacy laws, specifically the GP invadedrRegulation (GDPR), and raises significant concerns about user privacy and control over personal data.
