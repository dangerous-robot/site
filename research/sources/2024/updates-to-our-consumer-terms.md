Anthropic is updating its Consumer Terms and Privacy Policy to provide users of Claude Free, Pro, and Max plans with the option to allow their interactions to be used for improving future AI models. This update introduces a five-year data retention policy for users who opt in, compared to the previous 30-day retention period. The changes do not affect Commercial Terms users or API services. Users can adjust their settings anytime, and the updates emphasize transparency and control over data usage.
