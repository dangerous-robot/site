This CNBC article explores how major AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Google's Gemini, Meta’s AI models, Microsoft’s Copilot, and X’s Grok use user interactions for training their systems. While some platforms offer opt-out options—such as disabling data usage in settings—others, like Meta, restrict such requests to regions with strict privacy laws (e.g., EU/UK). The article highlights the opaque nature of AI training and the challenges users face in controlling how their data is used.
