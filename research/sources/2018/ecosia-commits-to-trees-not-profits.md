The blog post details how Ecosia's founder, Christian Kroll, legally restructured the company to ensure it remains a 'steward-owned' entity, prohibiting profit distribution or sale of shares. This decision aligns with Ecosias mission to prioritize reforestation over financial gains. The company urges other businesses to adopt similar ethical models for broader societal impact.
