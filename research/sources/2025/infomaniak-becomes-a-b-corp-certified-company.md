Infomaniak, a provider of digital services, has achieved B Corp™ certification, which reflects its long-standing commitment to social and environmental responsibility. The certification requires adherence to rigorous standards in governance, employee treatment, environmental practices, community engagement, customer relations, and transparency. Infomaniak’s practices align with its core values, including ethical operations, sustainability, and privacy protection, as demonstrated by its focus on renewable energy, open-source contributions, and stakeholder-focused governance.
