TechCrunch reports that Anthropic is implementing a policy requiring Claude users to explicitly opt out of sharing their conversation data for AI training by September 28, 2025. Previously, user data was deleted within 30 days unless retained for legal or policy reasons. The change affects Claude Free, Pro, and Max users, while business and API customers remain unaffected. The article highlights concerns about user awareness and the potential unintended consequences of the policy's design.
