Euria is presented as a privacy-focused AI assistant that operates entirely within Infomaniak’s Swiss data centers, ensuring no data leaves the country. It offers features like transcription, document analysis, translation, and reasoning while prioritizing speed, sustainability, and energy efficiency. The AI’s energy consumption is fully recycled to heat nearby homes, reducing environmental impact. Euria is accessible for free without requiring an Infomaniak account, although additional features require a subscription.
