This letter, signed by a coalition of state attorneys general, argues that tech companies misrepresent their progress toward renewable energy goals through the purchase of unbundled renewable energy certificates (RECs). It highlights discrepancies between the companies' claims and actual reliance on fossil-fuel-based electricity, questioning the environmental benefits of their practices. The letter also raises concerns about the impact of increasing energy demand on grid reliability.
