ChatGPTree, founded by John Vincent Lee, introduces a regenerative approach to AI by combining ChatGPT-like functionalities with an eco-conscious mission: planting a tree for every subscriber each month. The article highlights how this startup addresses the environmental criticism of AI by emphasizing intentional use and scalability of its positive impact. Lee, previously the founder of Loopie, frames ChatGPTree as a venture that integrates AI with ecological restoration efforts through partnerships like Evertreen for reforestation projects.
