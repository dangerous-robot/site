This article discusses a U.S. District Court ruling in *Johnson v. Dunn*, where sanctions were imposed on a law firm for relying on AI-generated legal citations. The court disqualified attorneys from representing the client and mandated publication of the sanctions, emphasizing the severe consequences of AI misuse in legal proceedings. The ruling highlights the court's growing impatience with AI-related errors and the inadequacy of monetary penalties.
