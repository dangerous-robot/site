This paper introduces a novel framework for benchmarking the energy, water, and carbon footprint of large language models (LLMs) during inference. The study evaluates 30 state-of-the-art models, including proprietary and open-source options, by combining API performance data with region-specific environmental multipliers. Key findings emphasize that while some models like Claude-3.7 Sonnet achieve higher eco-efficiency, others like o3 and DeepSeek-R1 exhibit disproportionately high resource consumption. The research also underscores the significance of deployment infrastructure, such as hardware and cooling systems, in shaping environmental impact. Additionally, a case study on GPT-4o demonstrates that even daily interactions can collectively impose a substantial environmental burden, comparable to large-scale human activities.
