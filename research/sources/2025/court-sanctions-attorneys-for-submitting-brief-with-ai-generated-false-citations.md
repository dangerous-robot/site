This article from Barnes & Thornburg LLP details a case where a federal court sanctioned two attorneys for submitting a brief containing numerous AI-generated false citations. The attorneys claimed they relied on an AI tool for supplementary research but failed to verify the accuracy of its outputs. The court ruled that the attorneys violated Rule 11, which mandates that filings must be legally accurate and warrant consideration by the court. Despite their claims of thorough review, the brief contained significant errors, leading to a $3,000 sanction.
