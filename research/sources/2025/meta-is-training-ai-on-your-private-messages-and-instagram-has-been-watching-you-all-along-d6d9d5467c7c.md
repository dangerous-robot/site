This article explores Meta's controversial practice of using user data, including private messages and interactions, to train its AI models. It asserts that Meta's approach to data collection undermines user consent by relying on oblique terms of service agreements and technically complex opt-out processes. The author draws parallels between Meta's AI training practices and persistent allegations of Instagram monitoring users through surveillance methods like microphone access and behavioral tracking. The piece also provides actionable steps for users to limit data sharing and protect their privacy on Meta platforms.
