This report examines the proliferation of AI-generated hallucinations in court filings since 2023, with over 712 documented cases globally, 90% of which occurred in 2025. It outlines the legal consequences, such as sanctions and dismissals, while emphasizing the importance of fact-checking AI outputs. The Illinois Attorney Registration and Disciplinary Commission (IARDC) provides guidelines on ethical AI usage, citing court requirements like The Illinois Attorney’s Guide to Implementing AI (2025). The guide aligns with Illinois Supreme Court policies, requiring lawyers to verify AI-generated citations to avoid risks like 'AI slop.'
