The California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (DFPI) reached a settlement with **Terrapass (Restitution Brands LLC)**, a Texas-based company, for failing to disclose over **40% hidden markups** on carbon credit sales to California consumers. The settlement included refunds totaling **$68,500** for affected consumers and businesses. This marks the first time the DFPI has enforced action against a carbon credit seller for undisclosed fees, ensuring greater market transparency in financial and environmental transactions.
