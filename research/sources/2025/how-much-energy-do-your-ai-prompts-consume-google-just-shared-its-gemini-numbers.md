Google published a technical paper outlining the environmental impact of its Gemini AI prompts, focusing on energy consumption, emissions, and water use. The article emphasizes the minuscule immediate impact of a single prompt but notes exponential growth in AI usage. It also highlights Google's new methodology for measuring broader factors like idle machines, data center overhead, and water consumption, which offers a more comprehensive view of the environmental cost.
