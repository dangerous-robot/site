The article examines the energy demands of AI, particularly during the training and inference phases. It cites estimates that AI could account for nearly half of global data center electricity use by 2030 and emphasizes the lack of transparency around its true environmental costs. Viro, the publisher, promotes its own AI service, **Viro AI**, as an eco-friendly alternative.
