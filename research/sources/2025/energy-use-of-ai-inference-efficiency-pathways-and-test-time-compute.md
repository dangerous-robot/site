This Microsoft Research paper introduces a bottom-up approach to estimate the energy consumption of AI inference systems, particularly for large language models deployed at scale. The authors argue that many existing estimates overstate energy use by failing to account for efficiency gains in real-world deployments. The study highlights that targeted efficiency interventions at model, platform, and hardware levels could reduce energy consumption by 8–20×, offering significant savings for large-scale AI systems.
