This article explores how AI misuse in legal practice, particularly fake case citations generated by AI tools, has led to judicial scrutiny for two attorneys in Texas and Pennsylvania. Judges demanded verification or imposed sanctions, highlighting growing concerns about AI's role in undermining trust in legal research and court filings. Legal experts warn that AI should not replace thorough verification processes in legal work.
