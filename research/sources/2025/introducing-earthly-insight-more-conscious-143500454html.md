Earthly Insight (EI) is an AI platform that integrates popular AI models like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude while prioritizing sustainability. It removes high-energy features such as image and video generation to reduce environmental impact. Additionally, EI plans to introduce real-time tracking for energy use, water consumption, and carbon emissions, allowing users to offset their footprint through the platform. The company aims to allocate a third of its revenue toward rewilding projects, which focus on restoring natural ecosystems. The platform is financially accessible, offering a free tier with limited queries and a premium subscription for deeper functionality.
