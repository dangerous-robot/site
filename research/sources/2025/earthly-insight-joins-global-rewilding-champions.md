The article details Earthly Insight's partnership as the first private-sector Champion for the Global Rewilding Alliance (GRA). This initiative pledges to allocate 33% of subscription revenue to support rewilding projects worldwide. Earthly Insight emphasizes eco-conscious practices in AI by avoiding energy-intensive features like image generation and focusing on text-based platforms. The partnership aims to accelerate global rewilding goals, including restoring biodiversity and protecting ecosystems. The Q&A section addresses concerns about AI's environmental impact and explains the alliance's pragmatic approach to engaging with tech for environmental benefits.
