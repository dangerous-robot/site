This blog post examines how frequently occurring LLM inference tasks, such as using ChatGPT, consume substantial energy, often far exceeding the energy used during model training. It cites studies showing inference accounts for up to 70% of AI energy use in some cases, emphasizing factors like model size, complexity of interactions, and hardware efficiency. The article also discusses potential mitigation strategies, including improvements in transparency, hardware design, and behavioral adaptations.
