This article examines Google's Gemini report on the energy consumption of AI prompts, highlighting both efficiency gains and broader environmental impacts. It emphasizes that while individual prompts use minimal energy, cumulatively they contribute significantly to global emissions. Viro AI is promoted as a solution to offset AI's carbon footprint by funding renewable energy projects through user interactions.
