Anthropic will begin using user interactions from chats and coding sessions to train its Claude AI models starting September 28, 2025. Users of consumer products will need to explicitly opt in or opt out, while commercial products remain unaffected. The company asserts this move will improve the AI model’s responsiveness and enhance safety measures.
