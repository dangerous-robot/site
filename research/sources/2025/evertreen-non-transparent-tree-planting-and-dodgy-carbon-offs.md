REDD-Monitor investigated Evertreen, a company marketing reforestation and carbon offset projects while raising concerns about transparency, verification of claims, and the legitimacy of its carbon credits. The article critiques Evertreen's lack of clarity about project locations, monitoring practices, and carbon storage calculations. While Evertreen provided a rebuttal in response to the investigation, suspicions about the company’s credibility persist. Updates in August and September 2026 addressed some criticisms but left lingering questions.
