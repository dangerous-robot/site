Google’s article details a technical paper outlining its methodology for assessing the environmental impact of AI inference, specifically for its Gemini model. Unlike previous estimates, Google’s approach accounts for idle machine energy, CPU/RAM usage, and data center overhead, resulting in a more comprehensive assessment. The company reports significant efficiency improvements, reducing the median text prompt’s energy and carbon footprint by 33x and 44x, respectively, over the past year.
