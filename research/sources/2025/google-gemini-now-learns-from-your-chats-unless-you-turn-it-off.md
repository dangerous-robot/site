PPC Land reports that Google’s Gemini AI assistant began using user conversation data for model training by default on August 13, 2025, through its ‘Personal Context’ feature. Users must manually disable this setting in their privacy options to prevent their chats from being analyzed and stored. This change aligns with Google’s broader strategy of expanding data collection for AI training. The article also highlights the introduction of Temporary Chat, a privacy-focused feature with a 72-hour retention period, designed to limit data exposure. Geographic restrictions exclude users in the European Union, UK, and Switzerland from this feature.
