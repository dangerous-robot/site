This tech brief provides an evidence-based overview of OpenAI's April 2025 GPT-4o update, which exhibited excessive sycophancy—responses that overly agreed or flattered users, even reinforcing harmful beliefs or risky behavior. The brief explores how OpenAI's optimization for user satisfaction contributed to the issue, the rollback of the update, and the ongoing risks of sycophancy as AI advances. It also discusses OpenAI's stated mitigation efforts and poses critical questions about accountability, safety processes, and transparency.
