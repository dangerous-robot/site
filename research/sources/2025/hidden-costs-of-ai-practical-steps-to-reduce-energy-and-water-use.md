The article explores how AI’s infrastructure—particularly inference tasks such as image generation and complex reasoning—consumes significant energy and water resources. It emphasizes that efficiency alone won’t offset the growing environmental footprint and advocates for mindful AI use, including opting for energy-efficient models and refining prompt design to minimize waste.
