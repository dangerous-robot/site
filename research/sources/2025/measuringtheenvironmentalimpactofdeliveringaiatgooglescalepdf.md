This Google-led study proposes and applies a comprehensive methodology to measure the energy usage, carbon emissions, and water consumption of AI inference workloads in a large-scale production environment. By instrumenting Google’s AI infrastructure for serving the Gemini AI assistant, the authors find that the environmental footprint of AI prompting is far lower than previously estimated. The results highlight the importance of full-stack measurement approaches to accurately assess and incentivize efficiency gains.
