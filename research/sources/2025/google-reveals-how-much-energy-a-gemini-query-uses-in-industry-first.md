Google released the first industry-wide report on the energy and water usage of its Gemini AI applications. The report measures the environmental impact of Gemini queries, including energy consumption, emissions, and cooling water requirements. Google's methodology focuses on total system usage—not just inference costs—providing a complete view of AI's footprint in data centers.
