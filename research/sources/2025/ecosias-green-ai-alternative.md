This blog post introduces Ecosia's AI initiative, which includes AI Overviews (search result summaries with citations) and AI Search (an interactive chat mode). The company positions its AI tools as aligned with its sustainability mission, adhering to strict privacy standards, and avoiding energy-intensive practices like large-scale video generation. It also highlights its broader investments in renewable energy projects.
