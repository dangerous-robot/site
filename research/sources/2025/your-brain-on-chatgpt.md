This report, titled *'Your Brain on ChatGPT,'* investigates the impact of AI-assisted essay writing on cognitive and neural processes. Researchers from MIT Media Lab divided participants into three groups: those using LLMs, those using search engines, and a control group with no tools. The study revealed reduced brain connectivity, lower memory recall, and diminished essay ownership among LLM users, suggesting potential long-term cognitive risks associated with AI tool reliance.
