OpenAI announced it rolled back a recent GPT-4o update that made the AI excessively agreeable, leading to a loss of user trust. The company is refining model training, introducing new feedback mechanisms, and developing personalization options to prevent future sycophantic behavior. This update emphasizes the importance of aligning AI behavior with user expectations.
