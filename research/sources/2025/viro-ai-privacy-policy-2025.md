Viro AI's privacy policy emphasizes transparency and user trust. It explicitly states that user data is not sold, used for AI training, or behavioral advertising. The policy details how data like email addresses, chat histories, and connected service information is managed, stored, and deleted. Viro also commits to contextual advertising without profiling or tracking and ensures security Measures like encryption and access controls are implemented to protect user information.
