This report reveals that nearly three-quarters of teens engage with AI companions, blurring the line between friendship and technology. Teens use these tools for entertainment, curiosity, and even social interaction, with many sharing personal information. The report calls for stronger safeguards from tech companies, educators, policymakers, and parents to mitigate risks while fostering digital literacy.
