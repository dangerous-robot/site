The article highlights a joint statement by the CEOs of OpenAI, DeepMind, and Anthropic, signed under the Center for AI Safety (CAIS), urging global recognition of AI as an existential threat. The piece also contextualizes the growing public concern about AI risks, including polling data suggesting 61% of Americans believe AI could threaten civilization. It mentions responses from DeepMind and references regulatory efforts like Europe's proposed AI Act.
