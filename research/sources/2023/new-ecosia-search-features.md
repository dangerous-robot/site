Ecosia's blog post outlines recent updates to its search platform, such as diversifying search providers in specific countries to enhance result quality, introducing visual indicators for sustainable and fossil-fuel-dependent organizations, and adding rich search features like direct train and hotel bookings. The launch of an AI chatbot focused on climate action is also highlighted.
