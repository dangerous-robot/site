WIRED reports that parents and their children filed a class-action lawsuit against Meta in Chicago, claiming the company used Facebook and Instagram images without consent to train AI models like Emu and Muse Image, as well as an unreleased face-recognition system for smart glasses (NameTag). The complaint asserts violations of state biometric privacy laws, including allegations that Meta extracted biometric data from user photos and stored it without notice or consent. The proposed class includes millions of Americans affected by Meta's use of their images since 2021.
