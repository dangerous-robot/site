This report, prepared by Deloitte for Google, quantifies the economic, social, and environmental impacts of Google’s data centers in Loudoun County, Virginia, over a six-year period (2017–2022). It highlights contributions to the local economy, including annual GDP growth of ~$1.1 billion and support for over 3,500 jobs. The report also details sustainability efforts, such as commitments to carbon-free energy, water replenishment, and grants totaling $2.4 million for local initiatives like STEM education and COVID-19 relief.
