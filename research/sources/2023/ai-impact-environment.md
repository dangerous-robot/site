This blog post by GreenPT's co-founder, Robert Keus, examines the environmental footprint of AI, including its dependence on data centers that consume vast amounts of electricity and water, often sourced from fossil fuels. It also addresses the issue of electronic waste generated by rapid hardware turnover. The post highlights GreenPT's commitment to sustainability by leveraging renewable energy, optimizing AI models for efficiency, and adhering to strict privacy standards like GDPR. GreenPT's metrics, such as a PUE of 1.37 and WUE of 0.067, are presented as superior to industry averages, emphasizing their focus on reducing environmental harm.
