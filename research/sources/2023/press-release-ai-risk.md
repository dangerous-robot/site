This press release highlights a historic coalition of AI experts, philosophers, and scientists framing AI-driven extinction risk as a critical global priority. The statement was signed by figures like Geoffrey Hinton, Yoshua Bengio, Sam Altman, Demis Hassabis, and others from top AI labs and disciplines, emphasizing the need for proactive mitigation akin to international efforts against nuclear war.
