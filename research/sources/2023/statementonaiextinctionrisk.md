This Wikipedia article explains the **Statement on AI Extinction Risk**, a one-sentence open letter signed by over 100 AI experts, including luminaries like Geoffrey Hinton, Yoshua Bengio, and prominent CEOs such as Sam Altman. The statement, released in May 2023, argues that the existential risk posed by AI must be treated as a global priority. The article covers the reception of the statement, critiques from skeptics, and the broader context of AI safety advocacy, including its release shortly after a similar open letter calling for a pause on AI experiments.
