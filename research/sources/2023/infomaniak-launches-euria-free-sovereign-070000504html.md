The article details the launch of Euria, a free AI assistant by Infomaniak Network SA, hosted exclusively in Switzerland and powered by renewable energy. Euria prioritizes user privacy by avoiding data collection for AI training and features ephemeral mode, where no chat history is retained. It is tailored for privacy-sensitive sectors like healthcare, finance, and public administration, complying with GDPR and Swiss data protection laws. Additionally, Euria contributes to sustainability by recycling energy to heat homes, preventing annual emissions equivalent to 3,600 tonnes of CO₂. It offers functionalities such as document analysis, transcription, and voice interactions, all while maintaining local data storage.
