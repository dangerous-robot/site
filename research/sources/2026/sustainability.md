This page details Microsoft’s sustainability efforts in its datacenters, focusing on carbon reduction through renewable energy, efficient designs, and low-embodied-carbon materials. It also highlights initiatives for water efficiency, such as minimizing water usage for cooling and exploring closed-loop cooling systems. Additionally, Microsoft addresses waste reduction through circular design principles and ecosystems stewardship, emphasizing biodiversity recovery and environmental responsibility in datacenter operations.
