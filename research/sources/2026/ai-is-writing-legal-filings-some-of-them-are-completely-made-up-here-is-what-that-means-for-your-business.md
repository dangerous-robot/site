This article emphasizes the growing problem of AI-generated legal filings containing fabricated citations, which are being submitted to courts globally. With over 1,500 documented court proceedings involving such errors, businesses are advised to question their legal counsel about AI verification processes and ensure rigorous validation of legal documents. Courts are actively sanctioning attorneys for AI-related errors, highlighting the need for human oversight.
