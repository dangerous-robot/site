The Ecosia app is promoted as a browser search tool that reinvests ad revenue into planting trees and combating climate change. User reviews highlight its transparent approach to funding climate projects, renewable energy use for powering its AI and services, and privacy-focused encryption of search data. Some users express frustration over the introduction of AI features, while others praise its positive environmental impact and user-friendly interface.
