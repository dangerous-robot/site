ChatGPTree advocates for **regenerative AI**, aiming to minimize unnecessary computation and offset environmental impact by planting trees. Its product, **EcoRouter**, focuses on efficient AI usage by reusing knowledge and routing tasks to the most appropriate models. The organization has planted over **5,000 trees** as of summer 2026 and offers that structures like EcoRouter can be adapted for organizational use. Additionally, ChatGPTree encourages community participation through surveys, donations, and merchandise tied to reforestation efforts.
