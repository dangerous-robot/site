The **Earthly Insight** app is an AI chat assistant aimed at boosting productivity and creativity while emphasizing sustainability. It integrates powerful AI models like GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and claims to allocate 33% of premium subscription revenue to global rewilding efforts. Users can monitor their environmental impact through tools like the Eco-Tracker, and the app offers features such as AI memory editing, LaTeX support, and file uploads. It targets eco-conscious professionals, students, and those seeking responsible AI technology use.
