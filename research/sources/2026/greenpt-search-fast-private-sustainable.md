GreenPT Search emphasizes speed, sustainability, and user privacy by providing immediate web results without forced AI analysis or tracking. The platform prioritizes optional AI summarization, European-based renewable energy infrastructure, and GDPR compliance. It avoids ads and user profiling while aiming for high relevance in results.
