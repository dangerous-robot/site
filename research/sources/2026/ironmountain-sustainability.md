Iron Mountain details its commitment to sustainability in data centers, emphasizing 100% matched renewable energy, energy-efficient infrastructure, and certifications like BREEAM. The document also highlights partnerships with organizations like SeaQURRENT and the Clean Energy Buyers Association (CEBA) to drive innovation in clean energy solutions. Additionally, it outlines their targets for achieving net-zero emissions by 2030 and providing 24/7 carbon-free energy by 2040.
