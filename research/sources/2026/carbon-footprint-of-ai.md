This guide from GreenCalculus provides a detailed breakdown of AI's energy consumption and carbon emissions. It covers per-prompt energy usage, the massive emissions from training large models like GPT-3 and Llama 3.1, and the broader impact of AI on global data center electricity demand. The guide also explains variations in estimates due to methodological differences such as boundary choices and accounting methods.
