This report by Environment America Research & Policy Center examines how the rapid expansion of data centers is driving a surge in natural gas infrastructure, reversing decades of progress toward renewable energy. It outlines the environmental and health impacts of increased gas plant construction, particularly in states like Texas, Louisiana, Pennsylvania, and Oklahoma, while highlighting the slowed growth of renewable energy sources like solar and wind. The authors urge immediate policy action to transition to cleaner energy alternatives.
