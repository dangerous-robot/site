This page details GreenPT's commitment to sustainability, highlighting their use of 100% renewable energy infrastructure,ISO-certified facilities, and optimized computational models to reduce energy consumption. The report includes metrics like Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) and Water Usage Effectiveness (WUE) and explains how these contribute to lower environmental impact compared to industry standards. It also offers users the ability to measure and export their AI's energy use and CO₂ emissions for sustainability reporting.
