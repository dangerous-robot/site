Infomaniak presents itself as aampion of ethical and sustainable cloud services, emphasizing its commitment to European digital sovereignty. The company operates with a foundation ensuring its independence and mission Focus areas include data privacy, environmental responsibility, and alignment with Swiss and European laws. Infomaniak’s teams are entirely based in Switzerland, avoiding outsourcing and promoting local economic contributions.
