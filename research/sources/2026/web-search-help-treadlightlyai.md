This documentation explains how TreadLightlyAI's web search operates, offering clarity and agency over AI usage. Users can toggle between raw search results, AI-generated summaries, or automatic research based on their needs. Brave Search handles queries, adhering to its privacy policy, while AI model providers process non-search messages.
