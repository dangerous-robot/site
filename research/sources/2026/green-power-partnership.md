The page details how the **District of Columbia participates in the EPA's Green Power Partnership**, which incentivizes organizations to purchase renewable energy. It explains the program's reach, including over 1,300 partner organizations that collectively purchase over **17 billion kilowatt-hours of green power annually**. The page also provides resources and tools for entities interested in joining or benefitting from the program, such as the Green Power Partner Mark and contacts for assistance.
