This article from Fello AI provides an in-depth review of **GreenPT**, an AI chat platform that emphasizes sustainability, privacy, and European data hosting. It contrasts GreenPT's pricing, features, and environmental claims with competitors like ChatGPT, highlighting its OpenAI-compatible API, privacy-first approach, and per-chat energy transparency. Notably, the review clarifies that while GreenPT's hosting and inference are eco-friendly, the upstream training of its open-source models may not fully align with its sustainability branding.
