Euria is an AI assistant designed to enhance productivity while ensuring data security and privacy. It offers features like real-time transcription of audio and video meetings, document translation, summarization, email management, and secure searchability of scanned files. Hosted in Switzerland, Eusia operates under strict compliance with GDPR and FADP standards, ensuring user data remains private and is not used for training AI models. The service is integrated into Infomaniak's kSuite collaborative tools, making it accessible for individuals and businesses.
