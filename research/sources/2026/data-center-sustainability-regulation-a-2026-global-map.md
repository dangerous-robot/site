The blog post examines the divergence in global sustainability regulations for data centers in 2026, comparing four distinct models: the EU’s disclosure-based approach, Spain and Ireland’s renewable sourcing requirements, the US’s cost allocation focus, and Singapore’s incentive-based strategy. It highlights that each jurisdiction enforces unique compliance rules, posing challenges for operators managing facilities across multiple regions. The article emphasizes the increasing importance of verified energy and renewable-sourcing data to meet these varied regulatory obligations.
