The report provides detailed statistics on Brave Browser growth, including monthly and daily active users as of August 31, 2026, and breakdowns of verified creators across platforms like YouTube, Reddit, and Twitch. It also lists recent Basic Attention Token (BAT) purchases and the list of countries where Brave Ads campaigns are active. The format adheres to Brave's commitment to transparency in its programs.
