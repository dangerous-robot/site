The article highlights recent increases in fines and sanctions imposed by U.S. courts on lawyers who rely on AI-generated false citations. Since 2023, fines have escalated from $5,000 to over $31,000, with cases such as those involving the Sixth Circuit and New Mexico Supreme Court leading the trend. Courts emphasize the need for lawyers to verify AI-generated content rigorously to avoid ethical violations.
