This article discusses *Noland v. Land of the Free, LP*, the first published California Court of Appeal opinion addressing AI-generated fabricated citations in legal briefs. The court imposed a $10,000 sanction and emphasized that attorneys cannot rely on AI tools without personally verifying citations. The ruling highlights ethical obligations and judicial scrutiny surrounding AI misuse in legal practices.
