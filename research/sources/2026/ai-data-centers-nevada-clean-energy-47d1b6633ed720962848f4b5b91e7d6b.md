The article discusses how the rapid expansion of AI-powered data centers in Nevada is straining the state's electricity grid and threatening its clean energy targets. NV Energy, the largest utility in Nevada, warns that proposed data centers could require electricity equivalent to powering the entire city of Las Vegas three times over, potentially forcing reliance on fossil fuels. Some data centers, like the Switch facility, operate entirely on renewable energy, but others are turning to fossil fuels, posing challenges for meeting Nevada's goal of 50% renewable power by 2030.
