This article examines the shift toward renewable energy in the data center industry, driven by large technology firms like Google, Microsoft, and Amazon Web Services (AWS). It highlights their commitments to achieving 100% renewable energy by 2025-2030, their progress in reducing emissions through carbon-free energy, and improvements in energy efficiency metrics like Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE). The article also discusses the role of smaller providers like Switch and the growing market for sustainable data centers.
