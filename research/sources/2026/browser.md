This privacy policy explains Brave Browser's approach to user data, featuring details on explicit opt-in requirements for services like Safe Browsing and Sync, minimal data retention (e.g., 7 days for BAT-related data), and efforts to anonymize or avoid collecting personal information. It also covers advanced features like Brave Rewards, Brave News, Brave Wallet security, and Brave Talk encryption, along with section-specific data processing tables. The policy emphasizes transparency, user control, and adherence to privacy regulations like GDPR and CCPA.
