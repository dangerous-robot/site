This article critiques the estimated energy use of AI inference, citing studies like *Oviedo, et al (2026)* to challenge overestimates by emphasizing optimizations such as batching, concurrency, and model distillation in production environments. The piece underscores that standardizing energy measurements for AI tasks, rather than queries alone, is critical as agentic applications introduce complexities like tool usage and orchestration overheads. Efficiency gains of **8–20x** are possible through model, serving, and hardware improvements.
