This article details a class action lawsuit against Meta, claiming the company leveraged user-generated content (such as posts, messages, and behavioral data) to train AI systems without adequate transparency or user consent. The lawsuit argues that Meta's disclosures were buried in lengthy terms of service agreements and failed to provide meaningful options for users to opt out. The legal dispute underscores broader debates about data privacy, ownership, and the ethical implications of AI development.
