This article from Norton explains how Meta AI leverages public and chatbot data from Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp to train its AI models, heightening privacy concerns. It provides detailed steps for submitting objection requests to prevent data use, although these requests are subject to review and not guaranteed. Additionally, the article outlines methods for muting Meta AI notifications and emphasizes the absence of direct opt-out options for U.S. users. It underscores broader privacy risks associated with Meta’s data collection and offers alternatives like account deletion or limiting interaction with Meta AI.
