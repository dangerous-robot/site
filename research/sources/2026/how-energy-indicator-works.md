This GreenPT Help page details how the energy indicator quantifies energy consumption for model inferences, presented in watt-hours for conversations. It emphasizes that the measurement excludes broader factors like device use, network transmission, or original model training. The documentation also links to GreenPT's methodology and sustainability pages for further context.
