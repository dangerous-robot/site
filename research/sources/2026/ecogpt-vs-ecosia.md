This article compares EcoGPT, a standalone chat application focused on eco-friendly AI with tree-planting funding, and Ecosia AI, the chat and search feature embedded in the Ecosia search engine. The piece emphasizes Ecosia's long-term tree-planting track record and EcoGPT's direct link between chat usage and tree funding.
