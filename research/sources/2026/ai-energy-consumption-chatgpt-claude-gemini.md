This EcoFlow article examines the energy consumption of popular AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini in 2026. It highlights how individual queries require fractional watt-hours, but collective usage strains data centers, potentially changing grid demand and utility costs. The piece also suggests ways to mitigate personal energy footprints, such as using portable power stations or solar generators, and compares AI energy use to household electricity consumption.
