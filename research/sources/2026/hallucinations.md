This database compiles legal cases involving AI-generated hallucinations, focusing on instances where courts or tribunals explicitly addressed or implied reliance on fabricated content, such as fake legal citations. It includes cases worldwide, with updates as new examples emerge, and is accompanied by an automated reference checker tool called PelAIkan to detect such hallucinations. The dataset has been featured in media outlets and is licensed under CC BY 4.0.
