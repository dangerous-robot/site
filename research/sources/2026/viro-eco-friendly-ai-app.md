Viro is an AI chat application designed to minimize environmental impact by using renewable energy and committing profits to climate restoration initiatives, such as supporting The Ocean Cleanup. It emphasizes privacy by avoiding data selling and training of AI models with user conversations. The app integrates with tools like Google Drive and Notion, offers customizable AI personas, and provides features like image generation and real-time web search.

Viro is notable for its published energy methodology, which measures real usage data rather than estimates, and its adherence to eco-friendly practices while providing a highly functional AI tool.
