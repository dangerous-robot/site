This article examines a lawyer sanctioned for submitting AI-generated legal documents containing fake citations. It documents cases where penalties ranged from monetary fines ($1,000–$50,000, with 45% of cases falling in the $1,000–$5,000 range) to disciplinary referrals and outlines three common failure modes: fabricated authority, misrepresented holdings, and false quotations. The article emphasizes the importance of verifying citations manually, including checking full text, pinpoint pages, and string searches against opinions.
