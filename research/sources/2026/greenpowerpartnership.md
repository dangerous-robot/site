The **Green Power Partnership** is a voluntary program initiated by the **U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)**. It encourages organizations, businesses, and communities to procure green power, defined as renewable energy sources like solar, wind, geothermal, and low-impact hydroelectricity. The program aims to expand renewable energy markets while providing environmental benefits.
