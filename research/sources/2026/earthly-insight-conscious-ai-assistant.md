Earthly Insight presents itself as an AI-powered tool designed to assist users with writing, brainstorming, and research. A key differentiating feature is its commitment to donations, where 33% of revenue from subscription plans is dedicated to rewilding projects, supported by partnerships like the Global Rewilding Alliance. The platform emphasizes Eco-conscious actions alongside AI functionality, targeting writers, creators, and professionals.
