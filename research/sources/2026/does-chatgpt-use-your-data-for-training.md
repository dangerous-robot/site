This article explains how ChatGPT uses user conversations for model training, emphasizing the **"Improve the model for everyone"** setting under **Data controls** in account settings. It also covers risks associated with **Temporary Chat**, **memory retention**, and **shared links**, including real-world examples like exposed Meta Muse chats. The post provides step-by-step instructions for adjusting privacy settings on both ChatGPT and Claude (Anthropic), along with cautionary advice on handling sensitive data in AI interactions.
