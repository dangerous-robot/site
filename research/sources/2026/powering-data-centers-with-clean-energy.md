This report by the Environmental Law & Policy Center (ELPC) explores the challenges and opportunities posed by the rapid expansion of data centers, which now account for a significant and growing share of U.S. energy consumption. ELPC discusses strategies like energy efficiency, flexible grid solutions, and clean energy sourcing to ensure data centers are powered sustainably without harming the grid or ratepayers. The document highlights specific initiatives, such as advocating for 'Clean Transition Tariffs' and working with utility commissions in states like Michigan, Illinois, and Indiana to implement equitable policies.
