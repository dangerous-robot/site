This page outlines Meta's comprehensive approach to sustainable data center operations, highlighting key achievements such as diverting 91% of construction waste from landfills in 2024 and using 100% clean energy for owned data centers. It also details innovations like low-carbon concrete, mass timber construction, and biofuel adoption to reduce emissions and enhance efficiency. The article emphasizes circularity in hardware practices, such as prioritizing recycled materials in server construction and expanding sustainability to network infrastructure.
