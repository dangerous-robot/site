Euria is described as a Swiss-hosted AI assistant developed by Infomaniak, a data-protection-focused hosting company. The tool emphasizes data privacy, transparency, and compliance with Swiss and EU regulations, using renewable energy for operations. While not end-to-end encrypted like alternatives such as Lumo, it positioning as a secure option for professionals in regulated industries seeking an AI assistant without Big Tech dependencies.
