This article provides a detailed guide to Ecosia’s generative AI features, emphasizing privacy, environmental considerations, and user customization. It explains how AI Overviews and AI Chat work, their availability across regions, and how users can disable or control these features. Additionally, it addresses the environmental impact of AI models and reinforces Ecosia's commitment to data protection.
