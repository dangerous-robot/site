The Green Web Foundation is an independent non-profit organization focused on accelerating the transition to a fossil-free internet by 2030. The homepage outlines its core projects, including tools like their **Green Web Check** for assessing website sustainability, the **Green Web Dataset** (an open resource for verifying green hosting providers), and the **CO2.js** library, which estimates carbon emissions for digital products. Additionally, they offer training, workshops, and advocacy initiatives such as the **Green Screen Coalition** and **Carbon.txt** for standardized sustainability disclosures.
