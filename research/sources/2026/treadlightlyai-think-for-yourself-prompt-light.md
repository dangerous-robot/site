TreadLightlyAI positions itself as a responsible AI assistant that prioritizes environmental sustainability and ethical considerations. It runs lightweight AI models on renewable energy, displays real-time energy and token costs for every response, and avoids energy-consuming features like image generation. The tool also directs 10% of its revenue toward AI safety research and advocates for transparency and accountability in AI development.
