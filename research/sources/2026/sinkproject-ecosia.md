This report evaluates Ecosia's climate and sustainability practices, highlighting its strengths in operational energy efficiency, biodiversity, and compliant profit reinvestment. Despite its high ranking in the SaaS/digital services sector, gaps in Scope 3 emissions transparency and dependency on external cloud infrastructure are noted as areas for improvement. The assessment is based on 13 publicly available sources, including audited reports and third-party certifications.
