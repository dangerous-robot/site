This Metir AI article analyzes the **Future of Life Institute's (FLI) AI Safety Index (Summer 2026)**, which evaluated nine AI labs using six safety and security domains. The findings indicate a troubling overall trend: **no lab scored above C+**, with Anthropic leading at **2.66/4.0**, while labs like **xAI, DeepSeek, and Mistral received failing grades**. The report highlights **weaknesses in existential safety readiness** (no lab scored above D+) and **industry-wide retreats from earlier safety commitments**, raising concerns about declining voluntary safeguards as AI capabilities advance. The article contextualizes the index as a governance audit rather than a product evaluation, emphasizing its role in shaping provider selection while urging caution against overinterpreting grades as assurances of deployment safety.
