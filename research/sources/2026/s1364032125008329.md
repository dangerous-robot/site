This report provides a detailed examination of the electricity consumption of large language models (LLMs), both in training and inference stages. It reviews current approaches to estimating energy demand, identifies key challenges in evaluation, and proposes comprehensive solutions for sustainable and efficient electricity use. The discussion includes hardware and software optimization, low-carbon energy techniques, and enhanced regulatory frameworks.
