The report, "Data Center Power Play," models the impact of escalating electricity demand from data centers, projecting a 60% increase in U.S. electricity needs by 2050 under mid-level demand growth scenarios. It underscores the risks of unchecked fossil fuel reliance, including higher costs, increased carbon emissions, and health damages, while emphasizing the potential benefits of clean energy investments. The report calls for stronger policy measures, ratepayer protections, and accountability for data center developers and utilities.
