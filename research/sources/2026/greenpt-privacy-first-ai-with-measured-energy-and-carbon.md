GreenPT positions itself as an alternative to mainstream AI platforms by emphasizing privacy, transparency, and sustainability. Unlike competitors, it does not use user inputs for model training and provides real-time energy and carbon consumption measurements for every request. The platform is built on open-source models and offers tools like chat, API, and document analysis, all designed to align with environmental and ethical standards. It also claims compatibility with other proprietary AI platforms through similar interfaces and integrations.
