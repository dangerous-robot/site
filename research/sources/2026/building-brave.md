The article explores Brave Software’s DevOps strategies for scaling browser development, release, and update infrastructure. It highlights challenges like automating builds, optimizing performance, and supporting multiple platforms (desktop, Android, macOS, and iOS). The team reduced build times significantly, leveraged cloud-based solutions, and improved update efficiency using delta compression. The goal was to handle 100x scaling for the growing user base of 32M+ monthly active users.
