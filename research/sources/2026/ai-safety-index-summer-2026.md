The AI Safety Index — Summer 2026 evaluates nine AI companies, including major U.S., European, and Chinese firms, on 37 indicators across six domains. It finds widespread deficiencies in existential safety and governance practices, despite variations in individual company strengths. The report highlights concerns like the militarization of AI, weakening pause commitments, and inadequate safety frameworks across the industry.
