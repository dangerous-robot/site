The article compares the energy consumption and CO2 emissions of traditional search engines like Google Search with AI-powered tools like ChatGPT. It emphasizes the significantly higher energy demands of AI tools, citing examples such as Google using 0.0003 kWh per query compared to ChatGPT's 0.0029 kWh (10x more energy). The environmental impact is further amplified when considering daily global usage, with AI tools potentially contributing to major sustainability challenges in the coming decade.
