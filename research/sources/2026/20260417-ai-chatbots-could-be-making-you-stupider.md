This BBC Future article explores concerns about the cognitive implications of relying on AI chatbots such as ChatGPT and Google Gemini. Researchers highlight reduced brain activity and diminished cognitive engagement when using these tools, with studies showing decreased memory recall and weakened critical thinking. The piece warns about the risks of outsourcing cognitive tasks entirely to AI and suggests the need for intentional and balanced usage to maintain mental acuity.
