This report by the Belfer Center examines the surge in electricity demand driven by AI-powered data centers, estimating future growth and analyzing its potential strain on the U.S. grid. It highlights regulatory challenges and case studies from Virginia and Texas, where rapid data center expansion is prompting divergent policy responses. The authors emphasize the need for standardized load profiles, transparent financing, and equitable cost-sharing mechanisms to mitigate risks like stranded assets and grid instability.
