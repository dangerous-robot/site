This NEXLAW AI article explores how AI citation errors have become a systemic issue in legal research, even after years of warnings. It categorizes four dangerous types of errors—fabricated cases, misapplied cases, fabricated quotations, and blended authorities—and documents over 1,174 sanctioned cases globally by early 2026. Sanctions have ranged from $10,000 to $31,100, including for attorneys at firms like K&L Gates and Ellis George. The piece also highlights the limitations of legal AI tools, emphasizing that human verification remains essential despite architectural improvements like retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Ethical guidelines and professional obligations now explicitly require lawyers to understand AI risks and verify citations.
