This article discusses the ecological implications of AI-driven thought processes, emphasizing the carbon footprint of generating new responses repeatedly. It introduces **EcoRouter**, a tool designed to reduce AI waste by reusing existing knowledge and routing queries to efficient models. The initiative also ties AI usage to reforestation efforts, planting trees as a byproduct of computational efficiency.
