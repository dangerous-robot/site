The article highlights how legal professionals are increasingly citing AI-generated fake cases, leading to judicial sanctions. Courts have documented over 1,400 instances of AI errors in legal filings over the past three years, impacting professions like journalism, academia, and government consulting. Over-reliance on AI, even when its limitations are known, poses risks across critical sectors, including high-stakes decisions like life-or-death scenarios. Research indicates that AI's guidance is uniquely prone to bias and that people may over-trust it more than human-generated advice, failing to verify its outputs adequately.
