This paper analyzes the energy consumption of AI inference, particularly large language models (LLMs), under large-scale deployment conditions. It introduces a bottom-up framework to estimate energy use per query, highlighting discrepancies between optimized production conditions and non-production estimates. The findings emphasize the necessity of efficiency improvements to manage escalating energy demand as AI inference scales.
