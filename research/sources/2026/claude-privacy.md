This article examines the shifting privacy landscape of Claude, Anthropic’s AI assistant. Originally designed with strict 'no-training' policies, Claude underwent a major update in September 2025, requiring users to explicitly opt out of data training or risk having their conversations retained for up to five years. The article contrasts Claude’s data practices with competitors like ChatGPT, highlighting its opt-in approach versus ChatGPT’s default training model. It also emphasizes the need for enterprise-level protections, such as Data Processing Agreements and Zero Data Retention, for handling confidential information. The piece underscores the risks of using consumer-grade Claude for work-related data and advocates for careful configuration of privacy settings.
