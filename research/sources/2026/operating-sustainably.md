Google’s report highlights advancements in sustainable water use, with 87% of freshwater withdrawals in 2025 coming from sources at low or medium risk of water depletion. It also details efforts to procure 35 GW of new clean energy since 2010 and achieve a fleet-wide average PUE of 1.09 in 2025. The company emphasizes innovation in carbon-free energy, AI stack efficiencies, and water replenishment initiatives.
