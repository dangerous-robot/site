The Union of Concerned Scientists (UCS) report, "Data Center Power Play," explores how clean energy can address the rising electricity demand of data centers while mitigating climate and public health risks. It highlights the threat of unchecked data center growth, which could drastically increase fossil fuel dependency, electricity costs, and emissions. The report presents modeling results showing that clean energy solutions like wind, solar, and battery storage are viable, cost-effective alternatives to fossil fuels. UCS advocates for policies requiring data centers to pay for additional costs, adopting transparent long-term planning, and supporting equitable access to clean energy.
