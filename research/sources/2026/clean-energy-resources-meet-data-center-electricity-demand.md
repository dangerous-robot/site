The blog post discusses how the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) is addressing the projected 15-20% growth in electricity demand over the next decade, driven largely by data center expansion and AI advancements. It highlights the role of clean energy resources, grid modernization, and energy efficiency in meeting this demand while ensuring grid reliability and affordability. The DOE provides a range of resources, funding opportunities, and technical assistance programs to support data centers and stakeholders in adopting clean energy solutions.
