This **IEEE Spectrum** article explores the energy demands of large-scale AI systems like ChatGPT, estimating its energy usage at **850 megawatt-hours per day** (based on 2.5 billion queries and 0.34 Wh per query). It warns of future demand spikes, forecasting **329 billion daily AI prompts by 2030** if current trends continue, alongside exponential energy growth for new data centers to support AI infrastructure. The article cites varied estimates, highlighting the challenges AI poses to global energy supply.
