This documentation outlines Viro's approach to estimating the energy consumption of AI queries, mapping it to renewable energy sources like solar, wind, and hydro, and funding climate restoration efforts. It emphasizes the focus on inference energy, token-based estimation, and avoidance of carbon offsets, while encouraging transparency and community feedback to refine its methodology.
