The lawsuit, filed in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York, names Google as the defendant for allegedly scraping millions of copyrighted books to train Gemini. Plaintiffs include Hachette Book Group, Cengage Learning, Elsevier, and author Scott Turow, who argue that licensing agreements did not permit such use for commercial AI. The case will test whether fair use applies to AI training and could set precedent for ongoing legal battles in the industry.
