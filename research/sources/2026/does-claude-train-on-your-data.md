This blog post by Strac.io examines whether Anthropic’s Claude AI trains on user data, clarifying key distinctions between consumer and commercial plans. Starting August 2025, consumer plans (Free, Pro, Max) train on chats and coding sessions by default, while commercial plans (API, Enterprise, etc.) exclude training data. Retention extends to five years if users opt in, versus 30 days if opted out. The post highlights risks where employees might inadvertently Share sensitive data on personal Claude accounts, recommending proactive redaction via tools like Strac’s browser DLP.
