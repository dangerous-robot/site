This documentation explains how to enable web search in GreenPT’s chat interface, craft focused questions to improve results, and critically evaluate sources. It emphasizes verifying information from publication dates, relevant passages, and original publishers rather than relying solely on provided answers. A brief video guide and step-by-step instructions are included.
