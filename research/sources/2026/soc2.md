This blog post from Brave Software details the organization's achievement of SOC 2 Type II attestation for the Brave Search API. The attestation, conducted by an independent third-party auditor, validates that Brave's security practices and infrastructure meet stringent industry benchmarks, including security, availability, and data privacy. Audits like this are increasingly required by enterprise customers to assess risk and ensure adherence to best practices.
