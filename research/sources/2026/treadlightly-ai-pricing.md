TreadLightlyAI's pricing page outlines four subscription tiers: Free, Starter ($3/month), Builder ($8/month), and Advanced ($20/month). Each plan varies in token allocations, file storage, and model capabilities, with a focus on 100% renewable energy usage for standard plans. The Advanced tier introduces Anthropic Claude models, which operate on non-renewable infrastructure. Users benefit from seven distinct thinking modes and real-time token tracking.
