This Brave Community discussion clarifies that Brave Sync servers are hosted on AWS (Amazon Web Services), primarily utilizing DynamoDB for data storage. The servers' physical locations are determined by AWS's infrastructure, and sync speed may be influenced by proximity to the nearest AWS region. Despite third-party hosting, all sync data is encrypted client-side, ensuring only authorized devices can decrypt and access it.
