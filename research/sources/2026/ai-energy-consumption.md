This report analyzes AI's growing energy consumption, citing data from the **International Energy Agency (IEA)**, **MIT**, **United Nations Regional Information Centre (UNRIC)**, and cloud providers like **Google Cloud**. It highlights critical trends such as the dominance of inference (80-90% of AI compute) over training, the projected rise in electricity demand for data centers (up to 50% of total usage by 2030), and the inefficiencies inherent in general-purpose generative models compared to task-specific alternatives. The report also discusses hardware innovations (e.g., FuriosaAI’s RNGD chip), water usage challenges, and actionable recommendations for reducing AI's environmental footprint, including optimizing inference efficiency and aligning hardware with renewable energy sources.
