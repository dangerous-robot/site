The article reports that US data centers consumed 183 TWh of electricity in 2024, accounting for over 4% of the country's total electricity use. Projections indicate a 133% increase in demand by 2030, posing challenges for policymakers and communities concerned about health, safety, and sustainability. The Kansas Health Institute report highlights the role of data centers in slowing the transition to clean energy due to reliance on fossil fuels and insufficient renewable sources.
